X01 What is not decided yet

The most honest sheet of the set. Every ⚠️ you left on the other pages becomes a line here, with an owner and a deadline. A decision with no owner is not a pending decision: it is a lost one.

1 How to write an open question

What is missing, who decides, by when, and what it blocks.

Four fields, always all four. The last one is what creates urgency: say what cannot be built until this decision lands.

2 The open questions

Ordered by what blocks the most.

Order by how much work each one blocks, not by how easy it is to decide. The list ordered by easiness is the list that never gets shorter.

3 What has already been decided

With the date and who decided it.

A settled question is not erased: it moves down here, with a date and a name. Six months from now somebody will ask why it was done this way — and the answer has to exist.
