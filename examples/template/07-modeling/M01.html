M01 The entities and how they connect

The data model in the shape of meaning, not of tables. Column names change; what an entity stands for in the business does not.

1 The entities

What each one IS, in a sentence the business understands.

One line per entity: the name, what it stands for in the real world, and who owns it. "Order" is not a table — it is somebody's intent to buy.

⚠️ If you cannot explain the entity without using database words, it is not a business entity yet.

2 How they connect

The diagram, and the rule behind every arrow.

The diagram, as text — so it falls under the traffic light like any other block:

erDiagram
  CUSTOMER ||--o{ ORDER : places
  ORDER ||--|{ LINE_ITEM : contains
  PRODUCT ||--o{ LINE_ITEM : appears_in

For each relation, the rule in words: "a customer may have no order at all", "an order has at least one item". The cardinality in the diagram and the sentence have to agree.

3 The dictionary

Field by field, what it is and what it cannot be.

Name, type, required, and the rule. "Tax ID: text, 11 digits" is the type; "Tax ID: of whoever pays, not of whoever is paid" is the meaning — and that is what most models are missing.
