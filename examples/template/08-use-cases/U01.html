U01 One use case, end to end

One sheet per case. The use case is where the other sheets meet: it uses the roles, passes through the screens, moves the entities. It is also the sheet that turns red most often — and that is the method working.

1 The situation

Who, wanting what, and why now.

Start with the person and the moment. "The nurse gets an alert at 7 in the morning" says more than "the system notifies the user".

2 The flow

Step by step, with what travels in each one.

The diagram, as text — so it falls under the traffic light like any other block:

flowchart TD
  A[the person does something] --> B{the system decides}
  B -->|happy path| C[what was expected happens]
  B -->|and when it does not| D[what happens instead]

Number the steps and say, at each one, what leaves where. The step nobody writes down is always the one that breaks at integration.

3 When it goes wrong

The unhappy paths, which are most of the code.

One per line: what fails, what the person sees, what the system does. The happy path is the least work; these are what set the deadline.
