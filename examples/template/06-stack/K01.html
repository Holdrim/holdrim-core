K01 What the system is built from, and why

This sheet is not a list of technologies. It is the list of choices, each with its reason and with what was turned down — because a year from now somebody will ask "why don't we use X?", and the answer has to exist.

1 The choices

One per line, with what it replaces.

For each piece: what it is, why this one and not another, and what you turned down. The rejected alternative is the most useful part: it shows there was a choice.

⚠️ A choice with no written reason becomes dogma in six months. If you cannot remember the reason, write "we do not remember" — it is honest, and it becomes an open question.

2 What it costs

Licence, operation, and the bill at the end of the month.

What is paid for, how much, and what happens if usage doubles. A cost that only shows up on the invoice is a cost nobody decided on.

3 What is left out on purpose

The list of "no", and the reason for each.

Technologies that were considered and refused. It stops the "why don't we use X?" conversation from happening three times with three different people.
