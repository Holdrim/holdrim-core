D01 Why this product exists

This is the first sheet, and the easiest one to write wrong: it tends to turn into a feature list. It is not. Write the pain here, not the cure.

1 The problem

Who feels it, when they feel it, and what they do today without you.

Describe the situation of the person living the problem, in one paragraph, without naming your product. If the sentence only makes sense with the product's name in it, it is still the cure, not the pain.

Say what that person does today to get around it. A spreadsheet, a phone call, a notebook. The toughest competitor any new product has is the way things are done now.

2 Who it is for

Real people, named by role — not "the user".

List two to four roles. For each one: what that person wants to finish, and in how long. "Wants to see the report" is not an end; "wants to know whether the payment can be released" is.

⚠️ If a role shows up here and does not show up on the roles sheet (P01), one of the two is wrong.

3 What changes if it works

The measured outcome, not the delivery.

One sentence per outcome, with a number when there is one. With no number, write "no measure yet" — it is honest, and it becomes an open question on the decisions sheet.
