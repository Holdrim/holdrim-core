R02 The rules of a sale

Every rule here is one sentence that can only be read one way. When a rule changes, everything that depends on it turns red until someone checks it again.

1 Price and payment

The price of a product is the one in the supplier's catalogue on the day of the sale (R04). The cashier never types a price.

A sale is closed only when it is paid in full. An unpaid sale does not reduce the stock and does not appear in the day's total.

2 Returns

A customer may return a product within 14 days of the sale, with the receipt, and gets the money back in the way they paid.

When a receipt is older than the return period, the till refuses the return and says why, so the cashier never has to argue about dates.
