R04 The contract with the supplier

The till and the supplier's sales system are two systems, written by two different people. They connect by agreeing on this page — and the supplier keeps the same page on their side.

1 The catalogue the till reads

Every morning the till asks the supplier for the catalogue. Each product comes with its sku (the supplier's code), its name, its price in cents and the date the price starts.

If the catalogue cannot be read, the till keeps yesterday's prices and says so on screen. It never invents one.

2 The order the till sends

After closing the day, the till sends one order: a list of sku and quantity, the quantities sold that day. The supplier answers with the order's number, which the till prints on the closing report.

Because both sides keep this page, a change to it on one side turns the other side's copy red until someone there checks it. That is the whole integration: two documents that already agree.
