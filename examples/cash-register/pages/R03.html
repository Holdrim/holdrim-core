R03 Closing the day

At the end of the day the owner closes the till. Closing is what turns the day's sales into numbers the shop can trust.

1 What closing does

Only closed sales count (R02, rule 1.3). The day's total is the sum of what was paid, minus what was returned.

Returns are counted on the day they happen, not on the day of the sale — which is why the return period decides how far back a day's total can still move.

After closing, the till sends the day's sold quantities to the supplier as the next order (R04, section 2).
