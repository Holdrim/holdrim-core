R01 Why the till exists

A corner shop sells a few hundred products from one supplier. The till exists so that every sale is charged at the right price, every product that leaves the shelf is counted, and the shop knows at the end of the day what it earned.

1 Who uses it

The cashier rings up a sale, takes the payment and hands over the receipt. They are not technical, and the till must never ask them to decide anything the rules below already decided.

The owner of the shop closes the day, reads what was sold and orders from the supplier. They are also the owner of this documentation: nothing here counts as true until they approve it.
