S05 Where it is going

The first version keeps documentation honest, for a whole team: roles, a graph of what depends on what, and requests made by voice or by sketch. What comes after it connects systems.

1 After the first version

A first case study: an internal system at a company that adopted Holdrim, documented in the released version and reviewed item by item, with the numbers published and the company left unnamed.

Then a protocol between systems built on Holdrim: contracts described in one shape, so two systems connect by reading two documents that already agree — a plugin, not a project.

2 Taking part

The engine is open source. The roadmap lives in the repository, next to the code, and an idea enters it the way everything else does here: by saying what problem it solves, in words nobody can read two ways.
