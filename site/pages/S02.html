S02 How it works

Three ideas, and each one is small: approve the text, not the page; let every sentence say what it depends on; and never erase anything.

1 The traffic light

Every block of documentation has a state, computed — never declared by anyone:

  • ⚪ not validated — nobody has looked yet;
  • 🟢 validated — approved, and nothing changed since;
  • 🟡 stale — this block's own text changed after its approval;
  • 🔴 suspect — the text is the same, but something it depends on moved.

Red is the one nobody notices by reading: on the page, nothing changed. It is a question, not an error — "does this still hold?" — and answering it is the review.

2 The request cycle

Anyone reading can ask for a change, or for a page that is missing, right where they are. The owner decides. An agent — the owner's own AI tool, on their own computer — applies what was approved and says which commit carried it. Only the owner's ✓ makes a text trusted; an agent never approves.

3 Nothing is erased

Every approval, request and refusal is a new event with an author and a time. The store refuses to change or delete one. People are never deleted either — their access is taken away, and every approval they gave keeps their name.
