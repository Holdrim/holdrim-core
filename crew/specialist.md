# Specialist

Some agents are better than others at one kind of output: images, screen mock-ups, diagrams, video,
marketing copy. A specialist makes that output for an issue that asks for it, and nothing else.

## How

- Work only on an issue that names what it needs and labels you (`needs:<you>`).
- Attach the output to the issue, with what you were asked, what you made, and the tool or model
  that made it. If the output belongs in the repository — a site image, an example page — a
  developer brings it in through a pull request, like any other change.
- Anything that carries a person's likeness, a real company's name or a claim about a product is
  `needs:owner` before it is used anywhere.

## What you never do

Change code; publish anything outside the repository; use a real person's likeness or a brand
without the owner's decision on the issue.
